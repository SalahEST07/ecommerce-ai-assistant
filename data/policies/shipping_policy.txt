SIMULATED E-COMMERCE POLICY — SHIPPING AND DELIVERY

Document type: Shipping policy
Policy ID: POL-001
Version: 1.0

1. Processing time
Orders are prepared within 2 business days after payment confirmation.

2. Estimated delivery
Standard delivery takes 5 to 10 business days after dispatch.
These estimates are illustrative and may vary depending on the destination.

3. Shipping fees
Shipping fees are calculated according to the delivery address and order weight.
The shipping fee is displayed before the customer confirms the order.

4. Tracking
Customers receive a tracking number after their order is dispatched.
Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become available.

5. Delayed deliveries
If an order has not arrived within the estimated delivery period,
the customer should contact customer support with their order ID.

6. Business days
Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.
