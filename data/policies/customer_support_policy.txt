SIMULATED E-COMMERCE POLICY — CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Document type: Customer support policy
Policy ID: POL-005
Version: 1.0

1. Contact method
Customers may contact support through the store's support form
or support email.

2. Required information
Customers should provide their order ID and a clear description
of their issue.

3. Response time
Support aims to respond within 2 business days.

4. Order information
Customers should use their order ID when asking about delivery,
cancellation, returns, or refunds.

5. Privacy
Customer information is used only to handle the request and
should not be shared publicly.

6. Escalation
If an issue remains unresolved after the initial response,
the customer may request escalation to a support supervisor.
