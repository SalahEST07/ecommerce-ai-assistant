SIMULATED E-COMMERCE POLICY — RETURNS AND REFUNDS

Document type: Returns and refunds policy
Policy ID: POL-002
Version: 1.0

1. Return eligibility
Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery.

2. Product condition
Returned products must be unused and include their original packaging
and accessories, except when the product is defective or damaged.

3. Return request
Customers must contact support and provide their order ID,
the product they want to return, and the reason for the return.

4. Refund processing
After the returned product is received and inspected,
the refund is processed within 7 business days.

5. Refund method
Refunds are issued using the original payment method whenever possible.

6. Return shipping
For defective or incorrectly delivered products, the store covers
the return shipping cost. For other eligible returns, the customer
is responsible for return shipping.

7. Non-refundable situations
Products that are used, damaged by the customer, or missing required
accessories may be rejected for return.
