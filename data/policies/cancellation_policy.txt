SIMULATED E-COMMERCE POLICY — ORDER CANCELLATION

Document type: Cancellation policy
Policy ID: POL-003
Version: 1.0

1. Cancellation before dispatch
Customers may request cancellation before the order is dispatched.
If approved, the customer receives a full refund.

2. Orders already dispatched
Orders that have already been dispatched cannot be canceled directly.
Customers may request a return after delivery, subject to the
returns and refunds policy.

3. Cancellation request
Customers must contact support and provide their order ID.

4. Cancellation confirmation
A cancellation is considered successful only after the customer
receives confirmation from support.

5. Refund timing
Approved cancellation refunds are processed within 7 business days
using the original payment method whenever possible.
