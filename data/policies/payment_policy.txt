SIMULATED E-COMMERCE POLICY — PAYMENT

Document type: Payment policy
Policy ID: POL-004
Version: 1.0

1. Accepted payment methods
The store accepts credit cards, debit cards, and bank transfer.

2. Payment confirmation
An order is considered confirmed after the payment provider
confirms successful payment.

3. Failed payments
If payment fails, the customer should verify their payment details
or contact their payment provider.

4. Duplicate payments
Customers who believe they were charged more than once should
contact support and provide their order ID and payment details.

5. Payment security
Customers should never share their passwords or full payment
credentials with customer support.

6. Refunds
Eligible refunds are issued according to the returns and refunds
or cancellation policy.
